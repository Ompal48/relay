HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, in the order they actually happen.

01 — DETECT

We check, not just ping

Relay hits your real endpoint on your real schedule and checks status code, latency, and response shape — not just "is the server up."

02 — CONFIRM

We wait for a pattern

One slow response isn't an incident. Relay waits for a threshold you set — three fails in a row, or latency past your bar — before it does anything.

03 — PAGE

We tell the person who owns it

Each endpoint has an owner. Relay pages them directly — SMS, call, or Slack — with the failing endpoint and last-known-good timestamp attached.
